# StockPilot — Product Overview & Feature Guide

StockPilot is an AI-powered retail inventory intelligence and demand forecasting platform engineered for modern multi-store retailers.

---

## 1. Core Value Propositions

1. **Eliminate Stockouts:** Proactive warning system alerts retail managers before high-velocity inventory exhausts.
2. **Prevent Dead Stock:** Identifies slow-moving capital tied up in stagnating products and suggests markdown discounts or supplier returns.
3. **Automated Restocking:** Calculates optimal Reorder Quantities (ROQ) considering supplier lead times, buffer stock, and projected customer demand.
4. **Natural Language AI Copilot:** Instant answers to complex inventory inquiries via an integrated LLM with live catalog data tools.

---

## 2. Key Modules & User Capabilities

### 2.1 Inventory & Catalog Management
* Real-time stock counts across SKUs and categories.
* Audit trail logging of every stock movement (damages, sales, returns, supplier deliveries).
* Low-stock thresholds and restock indicators.

### 2.2 Demand Forecasting & Intelligence
* 7, 14, and 30-day forward demand projections per product.
* Seasonality adjustments (weekend peaks vs weekday valleys).
* Safety stock recommendations based on historical demand variance.

### 2.3 Supplier & Order Workflow
* Supplier registry with lead times, minimum order quantities (MOQ), and contact cards.
* One-click Purchase Order generation from restock recommendations.
* Goods receipt workflow that automatically increments active stock counts upon delivery.

### 2.4 Scenario Simulator
* "What-if" inventory simulation sandbox: retail managers can model marketing promotions, sudden supplier delays, or price adjustments to observe projected cash flow and stock impact before executing real-world decisions.
